### Professional Profile

My engineering trajectory is driven by the necessity to look past high-level abstractions and operate on the fundamental principles of computing. I develop software adopting a "bottom-up" approach: starting from the study of hardware architecture, boot processes, and kernel fundamentals, I scale up to the design of databases, networks, and distributed applications.

My long-term professional goal is to specialize in systems engineering and the analysis of IT infrastructures, operating in environments where performance, cybersecurity, and deterministic resource control are critical architectural requirements.
